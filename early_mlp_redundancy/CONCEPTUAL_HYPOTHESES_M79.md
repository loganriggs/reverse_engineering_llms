## Priorities and stopping rule

The best-supported working account combines1,13,14,15: context-dependent local computation is filtered through a downstream-sensitive, nonlinear reader, and a reusable replacement must preserve background/change compatibility. Hypotheses3 and11 are the main unresolved positive explanations to pursue. Hypotheses16–18 are important alternatives and confounds, not excuses to optimize a single failed task.

Finish the fixed M79 comparison and close the chart-count/routing exploration. Its deliverable is a failure/success profile that informs supervision: geometric coverage alone is insufficient. The next stage should turn this into a concrete, predeclared training comparison for an executable input representation, using diverse competent tasks and joint background/change causal targets. First inventory the existing training targets and information access, specify a matched-budget baseline and an output-oracle ceiling, and verify that all seven evaluation families remain excluded. Select the architecture and tolerances before training; do not silently turn the output encoder into an input predictor by supplying native writes.

This is a research decision brief, not a frozen training protocol or a claim that a new representation has been trained. A new fit needs that protocol and correctness preflight first. Preserve reconstruction, contrast accuracy, output distribution, intervention fidelity, and genuine task discovery as distinct outcomes. No current result establishes a semantic manifold or newly discovered task.
