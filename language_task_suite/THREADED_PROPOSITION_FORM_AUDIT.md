# Threading semantic events does not fix the document surface

The proposition planner already has a `threaded` setting that focuses updates and limits repeated semantic moves. It had not been run through the full surface V3 audit: the form sampler forced 10–16 moves even though threaded plans normally draw 8–11, causing `Threaded plan exhausted legal moves`. The audit now uses the planner's intended move range for each mode, while an explicit `--fixed-moves` remains an override.

With surface V3 and threaded planning, the semantic audit passed 2,800 renders across seven document forms (canonical and typed lexicalizations), 7,404 answer sites, all targeted fact/source/comparison/measure/step/record edits that were available, and false-comparison rejection. This verifies consistency of the sampled plans and their visible answers; it does not verify a full next-token oracle.

The two 64-document, first-100-word typed panels remain far from the existing independent FineWeb development reference at offset 92,000. Threaded planning produces 371/368 word types, top-ten word shares .384/.389, and cross-document repeated-trigram occurrence shares .457/.455. The earlier independent V3 panels at the same random seeds produce 377/373, .382/.378, and .460/.459. The FineWeb reference has 2,432 types, .215 top-ten share, and .007 repeated-trigram share. The independent and threaded panels differ in sampled move counts and are therefore a diagnostic comparison, not a controlled causal estimate of threading's effect. Both expose essentially the same form bottleneck: the renderer repeatedly expresses the semantic graph with a small set of clauses.

Decision: retain threaded planning as a coherence knob and stop expecting it or a larger typed lexicon alone to repair natural-language form. The next generator change should replace the clause carrier with substantially more varied, corpus-grounded realization while retaining explicit semantic dependencies and independently checked visible answers. It must be evaluated on untouched natural windows and task-bearing windows before promoting it as a realistic language distribution. The [aggregate audit](results/document_proposition_surface_v3_threaded_audit.json) records per-form semantic counts and both form panels; the [earlier V3 reference](results/document_proposition_surface_v3_fresh_form_reference.json) records the independent and FineWeb comparison.
