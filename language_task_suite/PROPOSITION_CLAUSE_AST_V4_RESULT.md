# Clause-grammar V4 control: correct semantics, insufficient language variety

This is a bounded test of whether a typed clause grammar for the two most repetitive rule-site families could close the form gap. It does not. The [V4 grammar](document_proposition_clause_ast.py) provides six comparison frames and seven current-fact frames, including answers inside clauses rather than always at sentence ends. The document plan, semantic values, other tasks, and lexicalized vocabulary are unchanged from V3. The [visible-text parser](document_proposition_surface.py) reads the realized tokens rather than the latent plan.

The [paired audit](audit_proposition_clause_ast_v4.py) used the same 512 sampled plans and lexical seeds for V3 and V4 on each of two previously inspected FineWeb preview panels. All V4 sites passed visible-prefix answer reconstruction and complete-document parsing: 1,219 and 1,196 sites. Across the two panels, 1,264 false comparison/fact/update answers substituted into otherwise unchanged documents were rejected. One supported opening-color intervention per document was also replayed; 97/108 scored answers changed across the respective panels, and all edited documents and visible-prefix answers passed. These edits change the whole generated document, so they are semantic-propagation checks rather than isolated model causal tests. All six comparison and seven fact frames occurred in both panels. This is a semantic-integrity check, not proof that the sentences sound natural. The comparison baselines are descriptive and the natural panels are not matched by task.

The [paired family-site audit](audit_proposition_site_form_by_family.py) used one 41-word window per family per document and a size-matched random natural window from a different source row. Comparison site repeated-trigram share fell from .605/.625 (V3) to .492/.504 (V4), while natural random windows were .017/.014. Fact sites fell from .567/.562 to .490/.493, versus natural .015/.012. Numbered-step sites, which V4 did not alter, remained at .517/.508 versus natural .009/.002; their surrounding windows can change because neighboring clauses changed. The full figures are in [paired document 130k](results/proposition_clause_ast_v4_full_form_130000_n512.json), [paired document 140k](results/proposition_clause_ast_v4_full_form_140000_n512.json), [site 130k](results/proposition_clause_ast_v4_site_form_130000_n512.json), and [site 140k](results/proposition_clause_ast_v4_site_form_140000_n512.json). No raw source or generated passages are saved in these aggregates.

**Decision:** Stop extending this finite template family and do not train bilinear models on V4 as a purportedly more realistic language. Its modest lexical improvement leaves the dominant syntactic and discourse deficit. In particular, first-100-word sentence-start diversity barely changes despite 13 new clause frames. A manual surface check also found awkward constructions such as “a owner mismatch” and “records gave note a owner of Ava”; some inherited update wording remains equally stilted. The parser accepts these because they are semantically unambiguous within the toy grammar, which is a useful reminder that semantic checks and realistic language form are separate gates.

The next DGP implementation should change what is sampled, not just how a fixed proposition is worded. Sample a document-level latent topic/genre and discourse sequence; sample entities, claims, source relationships, references, and rule interactions inside that sequence; then realize each discourse act with a corpus-calibrated probabilistic syntax and lexicon. Preserve typed semantics so a visible-text checker can reject inconsistent assertions and evaluate selected rule sites. Compare the sampled marginal next-token law with the visible information available to a model; do not score against a hidden sampled state. The practical first milestone is a bounded grammar with multiple discourse acts and recursively composable clauses, a tractable prefix oracle on a small configuration, and independent form audits on a new FineWeb panel. Add Zipf and within-document burstiness audits, task-window as well as whole-document form, then train small bilinear models only after the intended rules have measurable predictive value and pass behavioral checks. A natural-corpus carrier can serve as a separate form control, but cannot be described as a full symbolic rule oracle.
