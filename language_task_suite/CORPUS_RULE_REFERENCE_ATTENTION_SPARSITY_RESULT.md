# Attention-weight sparsity on corpus-grounded list and bracket rules

The [ordinary-order transfer gate](CORPUS_RULE_REFERENCE_TRANSFER_RESULT.md) showed that two jointly supervised depth-2, width-64 bilinear models learned list and bracket behavior on fresh FineWeb documents. The [OV reduction comparison](CORPUS_RULE_REFERENCE_OV_RANK_RESULT.md) separated exact answer accuracy from teacher and causal fidelity. This experiment tests the user's iterated L1→prune→**no-L1 recovery** method on the same models and fresh panels. It is an attention-edge sparsity baseline, not a canonical full-attention decomposition or a discovered human-readable circuit.

The [sparsifier](sparsify_corpus_rule_reference_attention.py) keeps embeddings, both MLPs, and unembedding fixed and dense. Only the twelve `q1/k1/q2/k2/v/o` attention matrices are trainable and prunable. Starting from each dense checkpoint, it runs 100 steps of teacher-distribution KL on separate-rule **training** windows, with site KL plus 0.25 sampled-background KL, then globally prunes to 40% attention zeros; repeats at 60%; then runs 250 masked recovery steps **without L1**. The two arms differ only in whether the pre-prune steps add `1e-5` times the absolute active attention weights. Their example order, prune targets, optimizer settings, and recovery schedule match. The test panels are held out from fitting. Hard masks are enforced after every update and saved with local checkpoints.

Before running, the acceptance gate for each fresh panel was: list both-arm full-vocabulary top-1 and bracket both-arm correct-closing-character each drop no more than five percentage points; list and bracket edit-response mean absolute error versus the dense teacher each ≤2 logits; and teacher KL at sampled natural background positions ≤0.1 nats for each rule. These are explicit operational thresholds, not universal interpretability criteria. The two panels have 50/61 list and 1,533/1,532 bracket document pairs.

At the 60% mask, exactly **19,661 of 49,152 attention weights remain nonzero** in every run. Relative to the full 6,580,352-parameter model, this removes only 29,491 weights, or **0.45%**. Thus “60% sparse” refers only to the selected attention matrices.

The dense baselines have list pair exact .940/.984 (seed 71503) and .940/.902 (71504), and bracket pair correct-character .868/.856 and .802/.810. Final discrete behavior is therefore often near or above the baseline despite large mask-induced damage before recovery. At immediate 60% pruning, seed 71503 L1 list pair exact falls to .140 on panel 1, whereas seed 71504 L1 bracket correct-character falls to .442. The final no-L1 phase reverses most of these failures. A discrete answer score alone would miss the remaining edit-response errors around 2–3 logits and the background KL cost. **None of the four recovered runs passes the full predeclared gate on both panels.** L1 does not show a stable advantage over the matched zero-L1 prephase in these two seeds.

The [seed 71503](results/corpus_rule_reference_attention_sparse_seed71503.json) and [seed 71504](results/corpus_rule_reference_attention_sparse_seed71504.json) aggregates include every intermediate stage, both panels, all six gate booleans, exact/two-choice/character behavior, teacher site and background KL, and per-example causal-effect agreement. One seed-71504 attempt exited with native code 139 before writing a stage; a clean retry with one BLAS/OpenMP thread completed. The published result is the completed retry, not an average over crashes.

**Decision:** this attention-only pruning path is a useful baseline for preserving coarse rule behavior but has not established a smaller faithful causal circuit or interpretable sparse QK/OV paths. Stop coefficient tuning on these inspected panels. The next method comparison should include a gauge-aware QK/OV reduction and a genuinely whole-model or factored sparse representation at an explicit total nonzero budget, with independent rule families and the same behavior/causal/background gates. Circuit legibility must be assessed with actual path necessity, sufficiency, and reuse tests, not inferred from a zero count.
