# Natural sports-report carrier: realistic form, controlled score rules

The synthetic sports V1–V3 generators preserved their marked rules but were conspicuously templated. This arm samples **actual RotoWire report prose as a carrier**, then inserts one controlled score sentence and asks either for the winning score (copying) or the winning margin (subtraction). It is a bridge toward a variable, multi-rule toy language, **not** a full known-distribution generator: the human prose has no exact next-token oracle.

[`sports_report_carrier_dgp.py`](sports_report_carrier_dgp.py) partitions by game, checks that the source report's lead agrees with the box-score teams, score, winner, and weekday, removes the source score-pair sentence, and retains about 25 or 100 source tokens. It rejects other exact score, margin, and edited-answer digits, number-word point claims, and margin categories that a +1 edit would cross. It inserts a score sentence with the game's actual weekday. Each training/evaluation example has a paired version where the winning score and answer increase by one while the retained carrier stays fixed. The [audit](audit_sports_report_carrier.py) validates 300 original/edit pairs per partition, including the visible arithmetic, token sites, weekday, and game split. These conservative checks cannot certify every claim in the surrounding human prose.

The final 100-word screen leaves **554 train and 123 held-out RotoWire games**. SBNation leaves only 32 and 6 and is excluded: manual inspection found reports that contradict the box-score winner despite mentioning its score pair elsewhere. On 64 paired source games per RotoWire partition, first-55-word repeated-trigram shares are **21.7% in original versus 21.1% in controlled carrier** for train and **17.5% versus 21.5%** for heldout. Margin-site windows are more templated at **27.4%/31.4%**. The original and carrier first-55-word comparisons use identical games; target windows are a different crop. The synthetic V1–V3 64-document panels exceeded 50% repeated trigrams. The [aggregate audit](results/sports_report_carrier_audit_n300.json) gives all form measures and coverage.

The [trainer](train_sports_report_carrier.py) uses GPT-2 byte-pair tokens, full next-token cross-entropy with an explicit answer-site weight, paired score edits, and game-grouped train/validation/test partitions. The models are 1–4-block bilinear transformers. We gate weight interpretation on **both paired answer tokens being correct** and on the logit preference shifting toward the edited answer. Overall language loss alone does not show that the score rule was learned. The [fresh-checkpoint evaluator](eval_sports_report_carrier_model.py) draws new paired games from a selected partition. Checkpoints and raw reports stay local; only scripts and aggregate metrics enter Git.

At 300 updates and answer-site weight 50, the 1/2/4-block margin models all score **0% both-variant accuracy** on 64 held-out game pairs. Correct-direction shifts are 43.8%/54.7%/48.4%, with negligible mean logit shifts. A 2-block short-carrier **copy** model at the same exposure also scores 0%, despite 70.3% direction shifts of only 0.006 mean logits. This is an undertrained diagnostic, not a representational conclusion. The initial run with a donor template weekday was invalid; its checkpoints are archived locally and excluded. The corrected trainer has exact-resume checkpoints (tested by a bit-identical split/replay run).

With 1,500 updates, 4,000 paired draws, batch 16, and site weight 500, the 2-block models separate copying from subtraction:

| Rule and source carrier | Distinct train games | Held-out both-variant correct (64 pairs) | Fresh held-out both-variant correct (100 pairs) | Fresh edit-shift direction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Winning-score copy, ~25 tokens | 1,019 | 96.9% | 97% | 100% |
| Winning-score copy, ~100 tokens | 554 | 100% | 97% | 99% |
| Winning margin, ~25 tokens | 1,001 | 3.1% | 2% | 48% |

The long-copy and short-margin models were trained **after** the final edited-answer screen. The short-copy model predates that stricter screen, but its fresh held-out evaluation uses it. The fresh held-out panels remain game-disjoint from training; they may overlap earlier held-out panels. A separate 100-pair **train-group** margin diagnostic gets 41% full-vocabulary top-1, 8% both-variant accuracy, and 60% correct edit-shift direction. Thus its weak held-out behavior is partly a generalization/coverage issue, while even fitted games do not show reliable paired arithmetic. Copying works at short and long distances, so retrieval length alone cannot explain the margin failure. These comparisons do not prove a fundamental inability to subtract: only 554–1,001 source games, the score-pair distribution, query format, optimization, and output vocabulary may matter.

The aggregate model records are [300-step margin](results/sports_report_carrier_bilinear_depth1_2_4_300.json), [300-step copy](results/sports_report_carrier_copy_short_depth2_300.json), [short copy](results/sports_report_carrier_copy_short_depth2_1500_site500.json), [long copy](results/sports_report_carrier_copy_long_depth2_1500_site500.json), and [short margin](results/sports_report_carrier_margin_short_depth2_1500_site500.json). Their fresh-panel records use the same stem with `_fresh100` or `_heldout_fresh100`; the margin train diagnostic uses `_train_fresh100`. None of the margin checkpoints qualifies for a margin-circuit claim. The competent copy model is a suitable bounded positive control for comparing weight-only circuit ranking with full-path and causal ablation, after which work should return to varied, integrated, multi-rule language and DGP hyperparameter sweeps.
