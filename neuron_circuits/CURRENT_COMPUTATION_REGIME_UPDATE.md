# Current computation-regime update

## Sampling correction: controlled tasks are anchors, not the denominator

The concern that four clean tasks could dominate the interpretation is correct.
Even the later 17-family competence screen remains a human-authored probe bank. The
primary denominator is now a stratified random sample of ordinary FineWeb
predictions, and candidate mechanisms are discovered from causal residuals before
they receive semantic labels. The task bank is used afterward to calibrate and
falsify those candidates.

The resulting evidence hierarchy and coverage protocol are specified in
`BREADTH_FIRST_ONTOLOGY_PROTOCOL.md`. In particular, future model-wide claims must
report importance-weighted CE/KL coverage on the random natural population. A
collection of successful gender, sentiment, agreement, or retrieval examples is
not sufficient, regardless of how clean the examples look.

The first stratified census confirms that this correction changes the scientific
answer. Keeping every source edge on fresh FineWeb, stratum-specific rank-two
charts improve over a global chart by at least `0.05` in `143/414` layer-stratum
cells, and their mean split-half subspace cosine is `0.782` versus random p95
`0.616`. Four selected charts then gain `+0.259` finite full-logit fidelity and
`+0.179` signed CE-effect fidelity over the global chart on average.

The regimes are not all the same kind. Punctuation prediction at L12 and high-loss
prediction at L3 select genuinely specific charts on another fresh finite panel.
Distance at L6 instead forms a smooth family: adjacent-bin charts overlap much
more than nonadjacent ones, and a far-distance chart transfers better to the
mid-distance target than the chart fitted to that exact bin. L8 function-source
routes are metric-dependent: their matching chart is worse for preserving the
whole logit vector but better for preserving next-token CE. The emerging ontology
therefore includes categorical modes, continuous control coordinates, and
objective-relative charts—not a larger flat list of semantic tasks.

The cleanest newly discovered coordinate is not a punctuation-token lookup. At
L12, source-token identity has negative held-out R2 for both punctuation-chart
axes. Axis 0 still separates local from long-range routing within identical source
tokens (AUROC `0.712`), and restricting top examples to one edge per FineWeb row
preserves the profiles (mean cosine `0.940`). Its two poles remain at median
distance `2` versus `22.5`. The current operational reading is a context-sensitive
local-closure to long-range-formatting coordinate.

A tensor-factor red team further separates utility from identifiability. Crossing
the chart's route-read and head-write factors produces large finite interactions,
but principal-angle-matched random subspaces produce the same phenomenon. Hybrid
failure is therefore not independent evidence of learned QK–OV cooperation. When
the exact chart coefficients are instead permuted across native head identities,
L3 difficult-prediction binding survives a discovery-selected 4,096-permutation
hard null; L12 punctuation binding does not. Yet a larger 256-row L12 replication
still reaches `0.609` logit and `0.692` CE fidelity and beats the numeric chart by
`0.135/0.367`. The L12 subspace is useful and conditionally specific without being
uniquely canonical at the level of native head assignment.

## The four semantic probes are calibration cases, not the model ontology

The work no longer treats spelling, sentiment, gender, and tense—or the later
controlled agreement/retrieval tasks—as an exhaustive feature set. They are useful
because their counterfactuals are clean. They are vulnerable to selection bias:
finding an interpretable circuit for a hand-picked task says little about the
fraction of ordinary computation covered.

A label-blind FineWeb route atlas now supplies an independent check. Sixteen
geometric candidates spanning eleven layers were selected before decoding their
tokens. Ten semantic descriptions were frozen on one panel and tested on another;
nine passed, while all ten full profiles transferred with cosine `0.962--0.999`.
The replicated functions include document-boundary formatting, punctuation closure,
determiner/preposition relations, short lexical echo, long-range copy, and
name-valued association. These are mechanisms that the original four-edit suite
would mostly never ask about.

The remaining guardrail is important: these are reproducible descriptions of
high-magnitude route invocations, not yet proofs of sufficient end-to-end circuits.
The primary next metric is importance-weighted causal coverage on random FineWeb,
with incremental coverage reported as the vocabulary expands. That will show
whether a proposed ontology explains a large part of the model or only a curated
set of attractive examples.

That coverage audit is now complete. At the primary `0.70` membership gate, all
sixteen label-blind geometric modes contain `28.7%` of model-wide attention-edge
energy; the nine prospectively validated and named modes contain `11.5%`. The
semantic number remains `8.1--14.9%` over gates `0.80--0.60`, so it is neither a
negligible curiosity nor close to an exhaustive ontology.

Finite suffix replacement on fresh text sharpens the distinction. A rank-four
continuous route code recovers `0.804--0.984` of the full logit effect at four
selected layers. Named modes recover `0.059--0.813` and decisively beat 32
head-permutation nulls, but they improve aggregate CE only in the two earlier
layers. On a less cancellation-prone per-example metric, they recover signed CE
effects with mean fidelity `0.402`, versus a permutation-null 95th percentile of
`0.180`.

The working ontology is therefore plural rather than ambiguous: continuous route
coordinates are the faithful engineering description; recurring semantic modes are
sparse human-readable landmarks; and CE-adjoint/effect coordinates identify what
matters for a particular prediction. The next breadth-first pass targets important
residual computation left unexplained by the named modes, rather than selecting
another small list of familiar semantic tasks.

That residual pass now supplies a concrete answer. Known named modes explain only
`12--19%` of the frozen-route CE tangent. The remaining high-effect rank-four route
subspaces recur with mean cosine `0.914` and beat destroyed head binding. Much of
that structure is generic route covariance, but a rank-two complement still recurs
after the generic rank-four space is removed (`0.865` versus null p95 `0.697`).
The CE-specific gain is concentrated in layers 1, 2, 4, and 5 rather than being a
universal depth phenomenon.

Three operational pole descriptions survive prospective replication: local
punctuation/structure at L1, local current-token routing at L2, and current-token
punctuation-influenced routing at L4. A broad claim that all four L5 poles are
long-range fails, even though their richer profiles remain stable. This is a useful
example of recursive interpretation done conservatively: retain the stable axis,
reject the over-narrow name.

Finally, adding the frozen rank-two complement produces small but reproducible
finite improvements on two more fresh panels: about `+0.047/+0.052` full-logit
fidelity and `+0.060/+0.069` signed CE-effect fidelity. It ranks first among 33
subspaces twice, but by a tiny margin. The stronger result is a CE-selected residual
rank-four code with `0.924/0.933` mean finite fidelity. The best current unit is
therefore a compact continuous subspace plus optional human-readable poles, not a
flat list of task neurons.

### What the tensor weights determine by themselves

Symmetry-invariant random-function sketches of each head's two QK factors and OV
map produce stable weight-only rank-four bases in 15 of 18 layers. Stability does
not imply execution: after a matched best-of-four random null, only five layers'
weight bases align unusually well with CE-selected route spaces. A reconstruction
threshold that initially passed every layer was falsified as a nine-dimensional
chance effect.

A fixed FineWeb/CE correction clarifies what is missing. Two context coordinates
plus two weight coordinates recover at least 90% of CE-optimal route geometry in
15 layers; one context coordinate is sufficient at the 95% level only at L11/L15.
These are distribution-level coordinates shared across examples, not prompt-local
bases.

Finite intervention then changes the conclusion again: coefficient reconstruction
and suffix fidelity correlate `-0.303` across selected allocations. L15 is the
robust static regime under both full-logit and CE metrics on two fresh panels. L1's
weight basis is specific only for the full output vector, L4 consistently favors
the context basis, and L11 is context-specific for logits with less stable CE
specificity. Thus weights, natural routes, full outputs, and task loss define
different but useful coordinate systems.

## What the newest experiments changed

The exact Layer-8 tensor Jacobian had already shown that local mode selection
can be nearly exact when all later attention derivatives are included. The new
experiments localize and red-team that result.

1. A FineWeb-discovered global suffix triple, layers 9–11, reproduces the
   full-tensor-selected finite effect extremely well in the median: 0.993 on
   FineWeb and 0.983 on WikiText. It also reaches 0.974/0.952 fidelity to an
   independently frozen backprop-Fisher oracle.
2. It fails on a minority of sites. Median-only claims were therefore rejected
   as insufficient.
3. Exhausting all 84 three-layer subsets finds a good structured triple at
   almost every site: median 0.998/0.996 and 10th percentile 0.975/0.964.
4. A triple frozen for each tensor query does not reliably transfer between
   contexts. The route is more local than the tensor query label.
5. Simple selectors—singleton utility, leave-one-out necessity, and local
   derivative energy—retain high medians but still fail badly in the tails.
6. The sitewise result survives a strong multiple-comparison null. The best of
   84 Fisher-weighted random 16-mode programs reaches only 0.681/0.557 median
   fidelity, versus 0.998/0.996 for the structured attention triples. The
   structured route wins at every evaluated site.
7. Site-local additive and pairwise set functions only partially predict the
   84-mask response surface. Pairwise held-out R2 is 0.617 on FineWeb and 0.534
   on WikiText for finite fidelity. Their roughly 0.002 median selection regret
   is genuine rather than a flat-top artifact: random choice has 0.308/0.035
   median regret, and only 11–13% of masks lie within 0.01 of optimal.
8. Low-rank state routing is informative but not validated. With 32 FineWeb
   sites and grouped model selection it reaches 0.990/0.983 median fidelity but
   only 0.696/0.793 at the 10th percentile on FineWeb/WikiText. A cosine-kernel
   atlas reaches 0.812 OOD but falls to 0.609 in-domain. The failures are rare
   and severe rather than broad median failures.
9. Complete singleton and pair response tensors—not scalar summaries—reconstruct
   three-layer route responses almost exactly. Blind replication on new
   FineWeb and TinyStories preserves median and 10th-percentile fidelity 1.000.
10. The apparent catastrophic tail is primarily a discontinuity in our greedy
    sparse extractor. In 12 blind failures, fixed-support reconstruction has
    median fidelity 1.000, but independently optimizing the two near-identical
    metrics chooses supports with median overlap 0.25 and full-model
    infinitesimal effect fidelity -0.030. Shrinking the edit from 5% to 0.1%
    does not repair the discrepancy, while each support's own full analytic
    tangent predicts its finite effect at 1.000 median/q10.
11. The proposed cost-whitened stability certificate is falsified OOD: it
    rejects none of the blind failures. The previously localized ordered
    `[11,13,14]` residual—broad layer-11 heads funneling through layer-13 head 3
    and layer-14 head 6—is a real higher-order response term, but current
    evidence makes it a tie-breaker for our explainer rather than evidence that
    the native model enters a distinct singular circuit regime.
12. A symmetry-aware replacement works on the frozen failures. Exact and
    pairwise-reconstructed rank-16 sensitivity projectors have overlap
    1.000/0.997 (median/minimum), and their full-model finite subspace-removal
    effects have fidelity 0.999/0.992 (median/10th percentile). Thus the local
    operator is stable where the greedy neuron list is not.
13. Stability inside the route surrogate does not imply full native relevance.
    Route/full rank-16 projector overlap is high (0.94–0.95 median) and both are
    16–22x more causally sensitive than random subspaces, but route/full finite
    fidelity falls to 0.471 in the failure-group lower tail.
14. Contracting the projector with the current state explains that gap. Cosine
    between `P_route x` and `P_full x` predicts 5%-effect fidelity with Spearman
    0.867. Literal natural-component removal has the same tails, while the full
    literal component has roughly 4x random-subspace KL. Thus the active
    computation is better represented as a local operator applied to a state,
    followed through a downstream effect map.
15. Natural lexical edits distinguish generic operator reuse from semantic
    neuron reuse. Layer-4 leave-one-pair-out family routes are causal and
    head-specific, but their equal-data advantage over outside-family routes is
    only 0.024 on FineWeb and 0.016 on TinyStories, below the locked gate;
    pair-specific routes are not better. FineWeb uncertainty includes zero and
    pair advantages do not correlate across corpora. The robust reusable object
    is therefore a transport operator whose value/state supplies content, not a
    validated sentiment/gender/tense neuron or route.
16. Prospective new-pair validation confirms the generic interpretation.
    Correct-family routes beat wrong-family routes by only 0.014/0.016 with
    0.608/0.706 win rates, while one global old-pair route is as good or better
    and remains head-specific. A small family modulation is plausible on
    TinyStories, but the locked semantic-route hypotheses are falsified.
17. The synthetic grammar now separates computation from actuation. Calibrated
    generic answer effects receive 0.93–0.95 of the semantic patch's vector
    Shapley projection, while upstream interaction is larger.
18. Cross-initialization raw-channel alignment is not canonical. In MLP residual
    write space, same-manifest models align at Layer 3 with R2 0.624 mean/0.423
    minimum and mapped semantic fidelity 0.883.
19. Independently randomized answer codebooks break that unseen-relation
    alignment: Layer-3 write R2 is -0.490 and mapped fidelity 0.103, although the
    target's own answer interface remains at 0.906. The late interface is
    codebook-relative.
20. The independent 12-layer natural-language checkpoint rejects a universal
    one-ray output verb. A newline ray reaches only 0.464 oracle-scaled fidelity
    on WikiText despite a state coefficient predictor with R2 0.814.
21. A compact write module survives: FineWeb-derived rank 32 reaches at least
    0.80 fidelity on FineWeb, WikiText, and TinyStories, while random subspaces
    fail. A linear coefficient router remains insufficient OOD.
22. The weight-exact bilinear expansion reconstructs the natural edit below
    8e-7 relative error. Cross and edit-squared terms are individually
    insufficient but cooperate to give 0.81–0.88 fidelity in the rank-32 module.
23. The stronger cancellation and universal context-pairing thresholds fail.
    Anti-alignment is moderate (-0.36 to -0.48), cancellation ratios are
    0.52–0.63, and correct pairing improves fidelity by 0.18–0.27.
24. Execution is substantially native-axis privileged: a fixed 32-channel bank
    reaches 0.69–0.75 fidelity and 128 channels reach 0.77–0.84; random sets are
    near zero and per-example oracle selection adds only 0.01–0.03.
25. That compactness is boundary-specific. Four token-edit bases share mean
    subspace overlap 0.64 and their top-128 banks have Jaccard 0.75. One pooled
    basis and bank match target-specific versions, supporting a shared actuator
    backbone rather than operation-specific modules.
26. The required tail is behavior dependent. Mean minimum rank for 0.80 fidelity
    is 32/213/299/384 for newline/period/comma/` the`; 0.90 can require rank
    512–768 and 2,048 of 3,072 channels. Universal rank-256, rank-512, and
    top-1,024 sufficiency claims are falsified.
27. Exact carrier replay shows progressive compilation. After Layer 0, the
    changed residual accounts for only 0.35–0.39 of the full newline edit while
    repeated embedding injection carries 0.39–0.46 aligned credit; by Layer 10,
    residual-only fidelity is 0.994–0.997 and direct embedding/value-bus credit is
    about one percent. All-base/all-changed hybrids reproduce native forwards.
28. A frozen linear destination-state router is not universal. It reaches
    0.835/0.607/0.846 at the Layer-10 boundary versus rank-32 oracles
    0.881/0.817/0.873. FineWeb and TinyStories are partly solved from the input
    token embedding alone; WikiText exposes the token-identity shortcut.
29. The correct bilinear hypothesis class repairs the OOD failure. Thirty-two
    exact symmetric quadratic forms reproduce pooled coefficients below `1e-6`
    relative error. A shared weight rank-32 input space reaches
    0.798/0.705/0.819; a natural-state rank-32 space reaches
    0.812/0.746/0.808. Matched random spaces are near zero at this rank.
30. The compact program is relational rather than diagonal. A stable joint
    rotation leaves 55% off-diagonal energy and diagonal execution loses
    0.23–0.40 fidelity. Thirty-two frozen coordinate pairs recover the full
    restricted program; independent selections overlap 0.939.
31. The pair graph is a shared grammar, not a newline-only circuit. One pooled
    top-32 graph attains at least 0.85 relative fidelity for newline, period,
    comma, and ` the` on three corpora. Target-specific graphs overlap 0.64–0.83
    and do not materially outperform it.
32. Semantic axes and causal interactions separate. Random rotations of the same
    input subspace have higher median token-category AUROC than the canonical
    axes, so scalar monosemanticity is not identified. Selected interactions,
    however, beat random interactions descriptively and causally: their frozen
    label median transfers at 0.931/0.960 OOD, and they reconstruct
    0.920–0.940 of the pooled natural KL effect versus 0.594–0.718 for diagonal
    or random-edge programs.
33. The remaining graph error is predictable before intervention. The exact
    final-output Jacobian forecasts finite logit direction at cosine 0.997–0.998,
    ranks sitewise KL with Spearman 0.979–0.985, and predicts top-1 flips with
    AUROC 0.999–1.000. Hidden-delta norm alone has Spearman 0.342–0.532. Error is
    not confined to a few outliers: the top predicted decile contains 33–36% of
    total KL, below the locked 50% concentration hypothesis.
34. Four behaviorally transferable variables compile toward the prediction
    position at different depths. By Layer 14, destination-only projection is
    0.51/0.87/0.80/0.62 for spelling/sentiment/gender/tense. Equal-budget late
    windows beat early windows for every family, but a privileged post-boundary
    relay field fails the matched spread-position control.
35. Exact block factorials show one sharp fresh-attention construction event for
    gender at L12 (0.444 projection). The first 50%-destination crossings for
    spelling, sentiment, and tense are mainly carried-state events, with separate
    attention pulses at L9/L9/L15.
36. Those four attention pulses reduce to frozen native programs H8, H7+H1, H4,
    and H5 with held-out branch fidelity 0.945–1.000. State controls split them:
    spelling/gender heads dominate the entire attention delta, while sentiment/
    tense programs are specifically aligned with the semantic output quotient.
37. Exact source-role and QK/value factorials favor a verb-plus-payload ontology.
    Routing changes contribute only -0.07 to +0.02; changed values contribute
    0.98–1.07. Operand grammars replicate, but differ: direct cue reads for gender
    and tense, post-boundary reads for sentiment, and a broad relay field for spelling.
38. Large-corpus weight-conditioned atlases find stable rank-1 route geometry for
    L12H4 and L15H5, but human token categories do not beat frequency or shuffled
    controls. Their verbs are compact before they are cleanly nameable.
39. Leading singular modes of the native OV maps compress the semantic payloads at
    rank 2/16/1/1 per head. Rank-4 leading modes strongly beat matched random nonzero
    modes. Tense is the architectural exception: its selected invocation splits
    roughly 55/50 between current values and the persistent Layer-0 bus.

## Best current hypothesis

Layer 8 is not well described by one neuron, one fixed subspace, or one fixed
attention circuit. It looks like a sparse payload plus a local combinatorial
route:

```text
broad contextual state
        ↓  unresolved address computation
16 selected Layer-8 tensor modes (a causal intervention, not yet a sufficient payload)
        ↓
about 3 selected suffix attention Jacobians
        ↓
low-dimensional downstream effect
```

Layers 9 and 11 form a frequent backbone, but their partner changes with state.
The local route is structurally meaningful—it strongly beats matched random
program banks—but its selector is not yet understood.

This is compatible with locally concentrated causal sensitivity inside a
globally multiplexed model. It does **not** yet show that the naturally executed
computation is literally a 16-mode sparse program: the interventions identify
directions that matter, while sufficiency and semantic reuse require separate
tests. “Dense model” and “one small native circuit” are both incomplete.

The newest evidence adds a more important qualification: the local response
metric and the sparse explanation extracted from it are different objects.
Most three-layer route metrics have an essentially exact second-order path
description, including the blind tail cases on a fixed support. A tiny
higher-order residual can nevertheless flip a discontinuous greedy extractor
between causally different supports. The model Jacobian is smooth; our chosen
discrete explanation is not. A stable subspace or effect-family description may
therefore be more canonical than a single neuron set.

Across the new controlled and natural assays, the most useful generalization is
now a **computation-to-interface pipeline** rather than a single preferred basis:

```text
upstream evidence / address
        ↓  possibly local or nonlinearly routed
weight-defined operator bank
        ↓  state-dependent coefficients and cooperative terms
compact residual-write / output-effect interface
        ↓
task-codebook-relative decision
```

Different stages can have different identifiability. In the grammar model, only
the late write/effect interface aligns across independent runs, and only when the
random codebook is fixed. In the natural boundary circuit, a compact rank-32
write module is real and OOD-transferable, yet a single ray and a linear router
are insufficient. A stable fixed native channel bank executes most of that module.
Thus “neurons versus superposition” is not one model-wide choice: native channels,
distributed write geometry, and codebook-relative effects can all be the correct
objects at different boundaries of the same computation.

Even within the shared late backbone, description length is behavior- and
fidelity-dependent. Newline is a compact slice; common lexical replacement is
not. Future reports should give a rank/channel fidelity curve instead of naming
one universal circuit size.

## What is falsified or disfavored

- A universal native-neuron circuit theory.
- One fixed small suffix-attention module for all Layer-8 computations.
- Tensor-query identity as a sufficient route address.
- Three independent per-layer importance rules as reliable selectors.
- The explanation that exhaustive triples succeed only because many arbitrary
  programs collide in a low-dimensional output space.
- The claim that most local sites require all nine later attention derivatives.
- The claim that below-0.8 pairwise-program comparisons identify a distinct
  native singular-computation regime.
- The cost-whitened Gram-error threshold as a transferable failure certificate.
- Finite nonlinearity as the cause of the twelve blind tail failures.

## What is not yet established

- Whether a learned router can predict a useful local route from weights and
  state without inheriting the discontinuity of a sparse-support target.
- Whether a continuous sensitivity subspace remains stable where discrete
  greedy supports jump, and whether it transfers across contexts.
- Whether the useful route is best described by pairwise layer interactions, a
  low-rank tensor factorization, or a higher-order state-dependent rule. A
  simple unconstrained pairwise set function is now disfavored as a complete
  description, though it may still suffice to find one of many good masks.
- Whether the same atlas structure recurs at other source layers and in an
  independently trained checkpoint.
- Whether the three-layer route corresponds to semantic operations or only to
  behavioral equivalence at the final distribution.
- Whether the selected 16 modes are merely high-sensitivity interventions or
  are also necessary/sufficient components of natural computation.

## Next decisive test

Replace the discontinuous set extractor with the canonical local sensitivity
operator. Compare exact and pairwise-reconstructed top singular subspaces on
the frozen blind failures and matched controls, including finite interventions
along aligned directions. If continuous subspaces agree where sparse supports
jump, the stable explanatory object is a local metric/effect family rather than
a discrete circuit. If they also jump despite adequate eigengaps and exact
fixed-support reconstruction, the instability is intrinsic to the local causal
geometry. Follow this with literal-removal and retain-only tests before calling
the selected modes a natural sparse payload.

The first half of this test has now passed on the frozen failures. The immediate
red-team is native relevance: compare each stable route projector with the full
suffix-Jacobian projector and with matched random subspaces. Without that test,
the canonical projector is a stable description of the declared route but not
yet evidence that the route captures the model's complete local computation.

That red-team is now complete and mixed. The next decisive question is reuse:
does the full native operator or its state-contracted active direction recur
across contexts with the same linguistic operation, or is it only a local
adversarial sensitivity chart? Use frozen semantic counterfactual families,
compare native axes, global subspaces, and local operator directions at matched
rank, then demand held-out finite transfer. This separates reusable verbs from
context-local sensitivity without returning to unstable greedy supports.

The first natural transfer test now favors a generic head-specific broadcast
verb with weak, unconfirmed semantic-family modulation. The next high-value
replication is prospective: add entirely new lexical pairs and syntactic
contexts, freeze the current Layer-4 route families, and test whether the small
family advantage replicates. In parallel, use the synthetic grammar checkpoints
as a positive-control regime where the shared rule is known and native-axis
semantic transfer already passes. This directly tests whether reuse pressure
moves computation from generic transport toward axis-aligned rule operators.

The new-pair replication is now complete and favors the generic route. The next
cross-regime test should therefore use the synthetic grammar positive control:
apply the same operator/state/effect decomposition to pure-rule, mixed, and
arbitrary checkpoints. The key prediction is that a known repeatedly reused
rule yields a more stable state-contracted operator and stronger native-axis
alignment than either arbitrary lookup or generic language-model broadcast.

## Latest refinement: a sparse relational interface

The final Layer-11 backbone now has a more explicit intermediate
representation:

```text
native 768D normalized state
        ↓  ordinary-state rank-32 manifold
32 compact coordinates (axes not uniquely semantic)
        ↓  all diagonal terms + about 32 stable pairwise interactions
shared quadratic routing grammar
        ↓  pooled rank-32 residual-write backbone
        ↓  behavior-specific broad tail + final RMSNorm/unembedding
```

This is “computation in superposition” in a tractable sense. The coordinates are
distributed and freely decodable under many rotations, but the restricted
tensor has a sparse relational program whose finite effects transfer. It is not
opaque merely because no rotation makes every useful operation one-coordinate-
at-a-time. The next decisive test is upstream provenance: predict the high-degree
hub and edge activations from frozen Layer-10 MLP and Layer-11 QK/value paths on
unseen corpora.

## October 20 continuation: from sparse endpoint graph to upstream local charts

The sparse final interaction graph is real, but tracing it backward changes its
interpretation. The same axes and edge products are not cleanly present at
middle depth. Their readability grows gradually, becoming almost exact only by
Layer 10. Finite interventions show that this is causal transport, but named
high-degree hubs are not privileged carriers: random rotations of the same
subspace travel nearly as well.

At Layer 6, the correct local object is a state-specific transport Jacobian.
A single global map reaches about 0.79 fidelity; a Jacobian measured at the
actual site reaches 0.9999. Attempts to predict that Jacobian from a few local
context variables, or even 128 linear PCA coordinates of the full local state,
plateau around 0.85–0.87.

Exact operator hybrids locate the variation primarily in the bilinear MLPs,
not attention. Composing the analytic RMSNorm and bilinear derivatives directly
from weights reproduces the finite MLP route essentially exactly and the native
route at about 0.98. This remains above 0.95 for one-sided interventions nearly
two natural coordinate standard deviations large. The explanation is therefore
mechanistic and finite, not just a probe or infinitesimal Taylor slogan.

The Jacobian family has outliers plus bulk. It has a clear leading spectral
spike relative to shuffled marginals, but effective rank about 155 and no
32- or 64-mode near-complete dictionary. The current model is best summarized as:

```text
natural state
  -> broad but structured family of activation-conditioned local Jacobians
  -> residual identity + distributed composition of bilinear MLP maps
  -> late rank-32 interface with a sparse pair-interaction grammar
  -> broad output tail and logits
```

This is neither neuron monosemanticity nor arbitrary opaque superposition. It is
locally exact weight algebra with a partly reusable spectrum and a sparse late
interface. The unsolved step is to explain or classify which natural contexts
select the leading Jacobian modes.

### Semantic-axis and full-QKOV follow-up

The leading Jacobian subspace contains semantic information, but no tested basis
is uniquely semantic. Coverage-controlled PCA axes reach median simple-label
AUROC 0.74–0.78. ICA and varimax are worse, are not privileged over random
rotations, and ICA axes vary substantially across starts. In contrast, causal
reconstruction by the same top-16 span is exactly invariant to rotation and
predicts 87–89% of finite transport. This separates a real causal subspace from
conventional coordinate labels inside it.

The remaining native gap is now almost closed. A manual derivative of normalized
squared attention—including QK normalization and rotary, squared pattern
normalization, persistent Layer-0 value mixing, and OV projection—composed with
the RMSNorm/bilinear derivatives predicts finite native coordinate and late
quadratic-program changes at about 0.996 fidelity. Net QK target projection is
small (1–2%) but its norm is 12–15%, so it mostly redirects or cancels rather
than simply adding along the final response. Value/OV contributes about 3% net.

The present reverse engineering is therefore locally complete for a declared
route but globally incomplete. Given weights and the current activation, we can
predict the route's causal response. We still lack a short, stable account of
which language contexts choose which member of the Jacobian family.

## October 20 grammar cross-regime result

The same Jacobian ontology now has a controlled positive and negative case.
Pure reusable grammar has a compact early operator family (RMS-normalized rank
16–23), while 50–100% arbitrary load produces rank 125–160 on the same balanced,
jointly learned prompts. A legal bilinear gauge transformation changes the raw
rank but not the normalized result, and finite interventions validate the maps.

The surprising part is that the mixed regime is global. Correctly generalized
