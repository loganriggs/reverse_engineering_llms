The leading current account combines cross-head source structure, a broad behavioral spectrum, and imperfect estimation of both source moments and receiver sensitivity (1–4). It is more supported than a mean-offset or strongly nonlinear explanation of the present gap (5–6). Natural context dependence remains a meaningful unresolved alternative (7–12). Neither stable unique axes nor complete executable computational units follows (14–15,20).

Highest-information next step is H521: a fresh, frozen comparison of1024-context Fisher with64-context Fisher and PCA at ranks36/144/576. It tests whether the leading metric-estimation explanation produces reproducible behavioral improvement, including whether the smallest-rank gain survives. If gains fail, prioritize metric influence and independent training-pool repeats before adding complexity. If gains hold but remain modest, the next distinction is source covariance estimation versus context-conditioned weighting; a fixed-metric source-calibration curve is the cleaner first control. The KL-versus-CE objective comparison remains a separate planned branch, not a claim established by descriptive CE measurements.
