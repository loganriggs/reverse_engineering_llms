### Priority and next step

The leading geometric explanations remain continuous structure and mixtures of contextual states. The strongest immediate alternatives are nuisance context, recipient-dependent sensitivity, and nonspecific effects of editing. Gate-only, identity-only and simple radial explanations are weakened within the tested controls, not universally excluded.

Execute the frozen fresh-panel interventions next. Report all blocks and failed coverage, use native replay and zero-edit checks, compare common-norm near/far directions with PCA/random controls, and preserve native-scale transplant results separately. Measure full-vocabulary recipient change and donor attraction with baseline distances. Do not declare smoothness from a near/far effect alone: both mixtures and smooth manifolds can generate it. If causal locality is absent, stop promoting geometry to computational meaning and examine decoder choice or recipient sensitivity. If it is present, follow with tangent-versus-cluster prediction on additional independent occurrences and diverse competent tasks.
