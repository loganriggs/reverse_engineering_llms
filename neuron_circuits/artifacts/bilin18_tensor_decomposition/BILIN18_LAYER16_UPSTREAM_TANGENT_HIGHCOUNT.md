# Native Layer-16 channel support on naturally propagated upstream tangents

A natural rank-32 Layer-12 boundary basis is propagated through exact dynamic Layers 13--16 JVPs. At each site, the resulting Layer-16 state tangents replace the local PCA axes used previously. The Layer-16 MLP contribution to true-token CE is then decomposed exactly into all 4,608 native channels. Both marginal and coordinated greedy banks are frozen from discovery FineWeb.
