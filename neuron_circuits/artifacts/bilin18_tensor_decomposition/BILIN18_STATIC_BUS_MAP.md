## H739 — A fixed anti-identity/association map and a signed contextual coefficient predict the recall head's bus effects

We tested L13H0's persistent-bus pathway directly. The layer-0 bus vector for a token was recomputed from the embedding, the actual layer-0 residual mixing coefficients, RMS normalization, and layer-0 value projection. This is a position-independent token map before attention mixes contexts. It reproduced the captured native bus values.

For source substitutions where every changed position replaces the same token with the same other token, the bus edit factors exactly as delta_h=alpha*(B(after)-B(before)), where alpha=lambda13*sum(receiver routing weights at those positions). Multiplying the fixed bus-vector difference by the head output matrix gives a static residual-space direction. Its alignment with an unembedding contrast supplies a zero-fit sign prediction; multiplying by alpha predicts promotion versus suppression. This deliberately omits downstream normalization, nonlinear transformations and the logit cap, so it is not an exact or calibrated final-logit predictor.

All 90 task directions from H738 in capital recall, binding and copying met the repeated-token-substitution criterion. There were no additional structural exclusions. Sixteen capital Format A directions remain native-competence failures and are diagnostic; the other 74 directions belong to competent strata.

| Task | Native coefficient alpha | Static identity-map alignment | Result of coefficient times map |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capital recall | Negative (competent Format B: -2.47 to -2.09) | Negative | Promotes substituted country identity and the tested capital association |
| Entity binding | Positive (about 0.046 to 0.902) | Negative | Suppresses the substituted giver identity, favoring the alternative recipient |
| Sequence copying | Negative (about -0.950 to -0.232) | Negative | Promotes the substituted source identity |

The static task-answer contrast has the corresponding sign: negative for recall/copying and positive for binding, where the intended answer is the other person. The coefficient times this fixed-map contrast predicts the sign of all 90 observed bus-intervention effects, including all 74 competent directions. Within competent task/format cells, Spearman correlations between the raw score and observed effect range from 0.882 to 0.965. Signs were not fitted to outcomes.

Absent-token test: in every eligible receiver context, replace only the selected L13H0 bus contribution with each member of three fixed pairs—Kevin/Brian, stone/glass, and Canada/Australia—while holding the original prompt, local values and routing fixed. All pairs passed single-token and absence checks. Measure the corresponding identity contrast under injection of the first versus second token; for the countries also measure Ottawa versus Canberra.

The fixed map times the native coefficient predicts all 270 absent-token identity-effect signs and all 90 country-association-effect signs. In recall and copying contexts, injection promotes the corresponding absent identity; in binding contexts it suppresses it. The country-to-capital contrast follows the same sign reversal even outside factual-recall prompts. For example, the mean Ottawa-minus-Canberra injection contrast is +6.29 in competent recall Format B, -1.29/-1.01 in binding A/B, and +1.19/+1.96 in copying A/B.

This demonstrates reuse of the same fixed value/output map under differently signed routing on these contexts. It is stronger than featurizer reconstruction: the token substitutions were absent from the prompts and the predicted signs came from model weights plus native coefficients. However, there are only three fixed absent source pairs and one associated-country pair, with repeated use across correlated contexts. These counts are not independent generalization trials. The static map's negative identity alignment is established for the tested token contrasts, not for every vocabulary item. Association evidence is contrast-specific, not a claim that the map alone answers arbitrary factual questions. The absent-token effects are diagnostic counterfactuals, not task-correct completions.

An important remaining confound is operation versus surface cues. In the current panel, binding, copying and recall differ in syntax, source positions and final tokens as well as their intended operation. Family labels alone would also predict the observed sign pattern. Thus zero-fit sign success does not yet show that the routing coefficient tracks an abstract operation rather than a format or positional heuristic.

Audits: static bus reconstruction maximum relative error was 2.37e-7; the factorized bus edit matched the saved H738 edit within 2.83e-7 relative error. Recomputed original-token bus effects matched H738 within 6.68e-6; identity-edit error was at most 7.63e-6. All hashes stayed unchanged, effects were finite, and 720 full-model example forwards completed. No new natural documents or original reserved-test examples were used. The factorization rests on the independently checked H738 algebra and these direct native replays.
