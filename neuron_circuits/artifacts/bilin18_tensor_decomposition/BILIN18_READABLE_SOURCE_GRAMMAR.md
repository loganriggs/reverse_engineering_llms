## Guardrails

The predicate language encodes human prior knowledge and is therefore not a label-free discovery method. Frequency predicates are corpus-relative. Greedy variance reduction is not guaranteed to find the shortest or behaviorally optimal tree, and the leaves are evaluated after a separate route-selection stage. Split stability and causal fidelity are both required: a readable rule that changes completely with the sample is not a reusable model variable.
