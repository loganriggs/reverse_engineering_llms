# Preregistration: blind replication of regular and singular path regimes

Freeze the pairwise Mobius reconstruction, `<0.8` finite-effect failure label,
and the FineWeb-learned certificate `whitened Gram error <= 0.1159702614` before
examining new data.

Evaluate all 84 three-layer suffix masks at one tensor-query-selected site for
each of 16 queries on:

- FineWeb rows 17,000–17,299, disjoint from the current discovery/evaluation
  panels;
- TinyStories rows 800–1,099, a new corpus for this regime test.

Exact and reconstructed responses independently select 16-mode Layer-8
programs; exact finite interventions supply the failure label. Bulk replication
requires median/10th-percentile fidelity >=0.95/0.80. The frozen certificate
must cover >=90% at >=99% precision. Its rejected region must have at least 10x
the accepted failure rate. Failures must remain below 2% of triples and 20% of
sites to reject a broad higher-order regime.

No thresholds, sites, masks, or corpora will be changed after results. A corpus
with zero failures can validate precision but cannot validate enrichment; that
outcome is reported as not supported rather than forced positive.
