## H969 — Finite interaction removals prioritize attention5; early final-token cross products have little effect

Previous goal turn classified as progress: H968 traced exact native-trajectory contributions, but that additive accounting could not establish causal relevance. H969 removes the four-state interaction independently at all18 attention-output and18 MLP-output sites, always at the final token. For each quartet/state, edit is -a*n times that site's factorial interaction coefficient. Recompute the model from the original prompt with the output patch, allowing all subsequent computation to react. Attention patches preserve the native value bus. Identity and paired +/- Gaussian factorial edits matched to interaction norm accompany every site. The same random direction is used across a quartet's four states, with a*n signs, preserving constant and main-factor components just as the target edit does.

All96 locked H966 states evaluated. Rank sites by mean valence-aligned answer-margin loss only on the four familiar original-format quartets, the sole native-gate-passing cohort. No per-example correctness filtering. The other five native-failed cohorts are diagnostic only. This is an exploratory same-panel site ranking, not independent site-selection validation.
