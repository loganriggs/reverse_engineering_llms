# Repeated-embedding weight-JVP red-team preregistration

The first weight-derived path decomposition used four sequence positions and one cyclic donor pairing. This prospective replication evaluates the same locked decomposition on disjoint corpus rows, four unseen positions, and all three nontrivial donor permutations available in a batch of four.

The central claim is not that individual layer shares are universal. It is that repeated embedding injection is the dominant local path, the last three injections are minor, and the middle stack supplies a material part of the causal derivative. The red team separately tests donor, position, corpus, output-metric, local-linearity, and exact-recomposition dependence.

Thresholds were fixed in `configs/bilin18_repeated_embedding_weight_jvp_redteam.json` before these cells were run.
