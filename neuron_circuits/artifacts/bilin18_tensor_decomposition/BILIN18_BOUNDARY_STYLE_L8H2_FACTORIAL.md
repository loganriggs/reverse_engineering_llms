## Interpretation boundary

Layer 8 uses `mixed_value = -3 * current_value + 4 * persistent_bus`. Large opposite Shapley terms therefore indicate a native difference code, not two independent semantic features. The persistent bus supplies a stable lexical reference while the current value supplies its contextualized counterpart. The smaller residual passed by their cancellation is the edge payload relevant to the spelling intervention.
