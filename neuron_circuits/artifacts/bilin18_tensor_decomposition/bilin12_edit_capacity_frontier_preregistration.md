# Preregistration: capacity frontier of the shared late edit backbone

One pooled rank-32 basis and top-128 channel bank match target-specific objects,
but common lexical edits have substantial residual tails.  This assay measures how
large the shared basis and direct native bank must become before those tails are
captured.

Fit one pooled FineWeb write SVD over newline, period, comma, and ` the` changes.
Evaluate ranks 32 through 768, with equal-rank random subspaces.  Independently
rank native channels by mean absolute activation change times complete residual
down-write norm, averaged across the four discovery panels.  Patch their direct
writes without projecting them into the learned basis; evaluate 32 through all
3,072 channels.

Report the minimum rank/channel count crossing 0.80 and 0.90 Fisher fidelity for
each target/corpus.  This makes “compact” an explicit behavior-dependent frontier
rather than a binary label.
