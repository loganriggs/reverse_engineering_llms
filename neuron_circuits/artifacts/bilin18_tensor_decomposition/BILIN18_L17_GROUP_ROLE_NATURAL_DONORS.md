Hypothesis narrowing: a purely Gaussian-marginal explanation of H414 is weakened. Specific source-recipient pairing is now the leading explanation for head6's role within this family of interventions. Two limitations prevent treating that as uniquely established: donors come from a different fixed empirical pool (which previously served factor selection), and angle/norm matching transforms their natural direction distribution. This experiment deliberately breaks pairing but does not isolate it from every pool/transformation effect. Uncertainty is conditional on the donor pool. Moreover, the recipient residual already includes its actual head contribution; pairing dependence could partly reflect that structural relationship rather than semantic coordination.

Next decisive control: within the SAME H413 recipient panel, use six frozen document derangements preserving position and excluding self-donors. Compare both raw untransformed natural donor vectors and the norm/recipient-angle-matched versions. Raw derangements preserve the exact empirical head-vector marginal before model evaluation; matched derangements preserve the earlier geometry constraints. Freeze head6 loss contrasts for both families and their between-family comparison before outcomes, retaining head8/head4 role controls. If both families lose cancellation, this removes donor-pool shift and Gaussian sampling as necessary explanations; if only matching loses it, the matching transformation becomes central. Do not infer semantics even if pairing is confirmed. A later source-consistency test can then distinguish dependence on the source's own presence in the residual from dependence on the rest of its natural context.
