How many neuron changes flip a counterfactual?

Selected MLP activations from a minimally changed source prompt are patched into the original prompt. The crossover is the first tested and integer-refined subset where the source answer becomes the full-vocabulary top token.

Answer: under the answer-aware gradient × counterfactual-change ranking, the median strict flip takes 6.5 IOI activation instances, 8.5 agreement instances, and 3.5 bracket instances. At whole-neuron resolution the corresponding medians are 4.5, 8.5, and 3.5 layer–channel neurons.

What this means

The causal bottleneck is much smaller than the earlier sufficiency circuit: a few source activations can redirect the answer, even though reproducing the intact distribution requires a broad retained circuit. The ranking is pair- and answer-specific, so these counts are sparse causal handles—not a claim that the whole computation consists of only a few neurons.

Direction matters. IOI patches toward the first-mentioned name are generally easier than patches toward the second-mentioned name; changing is to are is also easier than the reverse in this sample. A handful of layer–channel neurons recur across nearly every pair, making them strong candidates for follow-up causal characterization.
