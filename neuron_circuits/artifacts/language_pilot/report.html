Language neuron-ablation pilot

The model was screened first, then neuron-position activations were retained or deleted under zero and a 50,000-window WikiText corpus mean.

Headline: task behavior is highly deletion-sensitive, but sufficient circuits are not tiny. Targeted masks need median 22.8–43.4% of activations under zero ablation, versus 89.1–95.3% for random masks.

Interpretation

There is a sparse set of highly necessary activations, but retaining only that tiny set is not sufficient. Corpus-mean replacement helps IOI strongly at medium budgets, while its advantage is smaller or reversed for agreement and brackets. Frozen-mask exact accuracy is materially higher than 90%-margin fidelity, so output-only transfer would make the circuits look more invariant than they are.
