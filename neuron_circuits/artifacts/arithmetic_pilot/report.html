How many neuron activations does arithmetic need?

A causal masking pilot on Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B, with graded numeric errors rather than accuracy alone.

Median 90%-margin circuit: 23.7% of 1,284,096 MLP activation instances using gradient × activation ranking. Across prompts: 2.0%–65.6%.

Collapsed across token positions, those masks touched a median 83.8% of unique layer–channel neurons at least once. The compactness is mainly position-specific.

Frozen masks stayed exact on 79.2% of changed problems; a shared 20% task mask reached 93.3% held-out accuracy.

What this establishes

The computation is compressible but distributed, targeted activations are far more important than random ones, and errors pass through meaningful numeric neighborhoods before severe ablation becomes non-numeric. The result is conditional on intact attention and does not make individual neurons semantically interpretable.

Method in one paragraph

We kept only prompts the intact model answered correctly, masked gated-MLP activations at every layer and token position, and recomputed the entire downstream network on every evaluation. We measured exact answers, answer margin, full-vocabulary KL, numeric distance, frozen-mask transfer, task-family masks, targeted deletion, and zero, task-mean, and 550,000-token corpus-mean baselines.
