Interpretation

Layer 23 by itself is not carrying the result. Removing the last four layers modestly affects typical IOI behavior and more strongly affects agreement and brackets. Removing the final token position is the sharpest test: typical IOI crossover grows roughly ninefold, yet 8/12 IOI pairs still flip with at most 100 earlier-position activations.

The million-activation outliers matter. They show that sparse source-position control is common but not universal. These remain answer-aware control interventions rather than certified minimal semantic circuits.
