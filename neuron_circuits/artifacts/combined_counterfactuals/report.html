How to read the counts

Each cell reports the median targeted units followed by the number of counterfactual pairs that flipped. An MLP unit is one scalar activation instance. Most attention units are 64-dimensional vectors, so the numbers compare causal handles, not equal-dimensional circuit sizes.

Representative mechanisms

IOI: recurrent MLP L15/N796 plus two concrete John/Mary message routes. Agreement: recurrent MLP L20/N4662 plus a single L19/H4 subject-to-answer message. Brackets: recurrent MLP L22/N1943 plus L20/H4 and H5 opener-to-answer messages.
